Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Mana Munch is a water, food and activity tracker with a cozy retro game on top. It's made by Mohammed Sheshtar, an independent developer in Kuwait. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • There's no account, and Mana Munch has no server of its own. I never receive your logs, your health data or anything else you put in the app.
  • Your water, food, weight and game progress are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Apple Health data is read, never written, and it never leaves your iPhone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Two optional features send limited data to other services, described below: food search (Open Food Facts) and AI meal estimates with your own Claude API key (Anthropic).

What's stored on your iPhone

  • What you log: drinks (amount, type and caffeine), meals and foods, weight, and the breaks you log in play sessions.
  • Your profile and targets: name (optional), date of birth, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, reminder times and settings.
  • Your game progress: XP, levels, quests, bosses, streaks and unlocked cartridges.

This data stays in the app's storage on your device, where its widgets and Live Activity use it too. Like any app's data, it's included in your iPhone's backups (to iCloud or a computer) if you have them turned on. Mana Munch has no cloud sync of its own.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Mana Munch reads your active and resting energy, steps, exercise minutes, workouts, activity rings, weight, body fat, height, date of birth and biological sex. It uses them to adjust your daily targets and to power the game: your steps, for example, damage the weekly boss.

Mana Munch never writes to Apple Health. Health data isn't sent anywhere, isn't used for advertising and isn't shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Mana Munch.

Food search and barcodes (Open Food Facts)

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, your search words or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to look up the product. Nothing else from the app is sent: no health data, no name and no identifier. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

The camera is used only on your iPhone, to read barcodes and nutrition labels. No photo is uploaded.

AI meal estimates

"Describe a meal" estimates nutrition from a short text you write.

  • Where Apple's on-device model is available, the estimate is made on your iPhone.
  • If you add your own Claude API key in Hero → Profile & Settings → AI meal estimates (off by default), your meal description is sent to Anthropic to make the estimate. That happens under your own Anthropic account and the Anthropic privacy policy. I never see the description or the key, which is kept in your iPhone's keychain.

The app asks your permission before it sends anything to Anthropic, and every estimate is labelled "Estimate".

Reminders, widgets and Siri

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets, the Live Activity and Siri actions such as "+250 ml" read and write the same on-device data.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Hero → Profile & Settings → Export data (CSV) makes a file of your logs that you can save or share. It goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Deleting Mana Munch deletes all of its data from your iPhone, though backups you made earlier may still contain it. Apple Health isn't affected, because Mana Munch never writes to it.

Children

Mana Munch is designed for adults (18 and over) and isn't directed at children. Its calorie and water estimates aren't meant for children or teens.
