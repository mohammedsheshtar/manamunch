Mana Munch العربية

A cozy retro RPG where your real day is the controller

  • Water fills your MANA, and meals are your MUNCH.
  • Apple Watch activity powers your STAMINA, and caffeine shows as a headroom meter.
  • Every log levels up your pixel hero and their buddy. Daily quests, a weekly boss and 10 cartridges that re-skin the whole app.
  • Kind by design: nothing rewards eating less, your buddy is never sad, and a missed day is never a disaster.
  • Your health data stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
