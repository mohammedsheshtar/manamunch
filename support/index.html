Common questions

How do I log a custom amount of water?

Hold the +250 ml button on Home for other sizes, or tap the MANA meter to type an exact amount.

Why don't my steps or workouts show up?

Mana Munch reads them from Apple Health. Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Mana Munch, and make sure the categories are turned on.

Can I hide calorie and weight numbers?

Yes. Turn on Hide numbers in Hero → Profile & Settings → Game settings. The bars and the game stay.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing bad. Shields, which you earn every 7 streak days, cover missed days automatically. You also get a planned rest day every week, and a pause (for when you're sick, traveling or fasting in Ramadan) freezes your streak and quests.

How do I change the look?

Go to Hero → Cartridges. Each cartridge re-skins the whole app.

How do I back up my data?

Hero → Profile & Settings → Export data (CSV) saves your logs as a file. Your data is also in your iPhone's backups.

Does Mana Munch work in Arabic?

Yes. It follows your iPhone's language.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no loot boxes. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

Wellbeing

Mana Munch is for adults (18+). It gives estimates for general wellness and isn't medical advice.

If food, weight or exercise ever feels stressful, please talk to someone you trust or a health professional. To find a free, confidential helpline in your country, visit findahelpline.com.

Privacy

Your health data stays on your iPhone, and there's no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
